How it works

From the van to a verified record

AI organizes and checks. People verify. Every step is logged with who did it and when.

Capture

Technicians work through a mobile checklist, add notes and photos, and collect sign-off. Times come from the server clock, not the device.

Organize

Record understanding maps free-text notes and untagged photos onto checklist steps and classifies each file by stage.

Check

Completeness analysis and exception detection flag missing photos, notes, sign-offs and inconsistent timestamps.

Review

An operations reviewer approves or rejects evidence and verifies the record — or sends it back with a request.

Report

A customer-ready report is generated from approved evidence only, clearly marked Draft until a reviewer signs off.

Evidence integrity

Submitted evidence is not verified completion

A photo shows what a camera saw at one moment. It doesn't prove a job was done correctly. RouteProof keeps these states separate everywhere — in the app, in reports and in disputes.

Level 1

Submitted

The technician entered checklist items, notes and files. Nothing has been checked yet.

Level 2

Checked

Automated analysis found the record complete against its template, or listed what's missing.

Level 3

Evidence reviewed

A named reviewer approved or rejected each file. Only approved files reach customer reports.

Level 4

Verified

A named reviewer confirmed the documentation is complete. That's a records check, not a site inspection.

What RouteProof will never do

  • Invent a timestamp. A missing capture time shows as "not available."
  • Fill in a signature or customer approval that wasn't captured.
  • Mark a record verified on its own. AI flags; people decide.
  • Treat a photo as proof that work met spec.

What it records instead

  • Server-side times for check-in, each step, notes, uploads and sign-off.
  • Device-reported capture times, labeled as device metadata.
  • File fingerprints (SHA-256) to spot the same photo reused across jobs.
  • Every reviewer decision, with name, time and reason.
